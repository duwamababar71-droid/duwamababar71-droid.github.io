01 — Launch System

Product launch, end to end.

Use this sequence for a new product, collection, accessory, apparel drop, or print-on-demand campaign.

Research the market

Define the buyer, use case, price range, competing products, search intent, and the reason your product should exist.

Build the offer

Create the product name, value proposition, variants, pricing, margin target, mockups, product photos, description, FAQs, shipping and returns information.

Prepare the store

Connect your domain, configure navigation, collections, checkout, payment methods, analytics, support email, policies, mobile layout, and tracking.

Soft launch first

Send a small audience to the product, test checkout, watch conversion and customer questions, then fix friction before scaling paid traffic.

Scale what works

Increase creative testing, retarget engaged visitors, build bundles and upsells, expand winning products, and review profit—not just revenue.

02 — All-Platform Playbook

One product. Multiple channels.

Use each channel for the job it does best, while keeping your product data, pricing logic, brand assets, and customer experience consistent.

STOREShopify

Best for owning the storefront, checkout experience, customer journey, analytics, and brand identity.

STOREWooCommerce

Useful when you want WordPress flexibility and deeper control over your site and content.

MARKETPLACEEtsy

Strong for design-led, personalized and giftable products where marketplace discovery matters.

MARKETPLACEAmazon

Use for high-intent marketplace demand, but keep listings, brand rules, margins and inventory carefully managed.

SOCIALFacebook + Instagram

Use Reels, product storytelling, creator content, retargeting and community engagement to drive qualified traffic.

SOCIALTikTok

Test short-form hooks, product demos, before/after concepts, creator-style videos and trend-aware creative.

SOCIALYouTube

Build longer-form reviews, how-to content, comparisons and evergreen search traffic around the product.

PODGearLaunch

Useful for print-on-demand workflows where designs are applied to products and fulfillment is handled by the platform.

03 — GearLaunch

GearLaunch setup workflow.

A practical checklist based on GearLaunch's current setup guidance.

STEP 01

Domain + email

Buy a professional domain, connect the DNS as instructed, and set up customer-support email forwarding.

STEP 02

Create the store

Create your GearLaunch account/store, submit the store for review, and complete the launch checklist.

STEP 03

Design products

Use print-ready artwork, strong contrast, readable typography, correct dimensions, and product-specific mockups.

STEP 04

Build storefronts

Organize products into clear collections/storefronts so customers can browse by theme, audience, product or campaign.

STEP 05

Pricing + upsells

Calculate base cost, shipping, platform fees, ad spend allowance and target profit before setting the retail price.

STEP 06

Test before ads

Place a test order, verify checkout and notifications, inspect the product quality, then start promotion.

05 — Launch Tips

Small changes, better launches.

Start with one hero product

Make the first launch easy to understand. Expand after you see real demand.

Make creative before launch

Prepare multiple hooks, thumbnails, short videos, product shots and testimonials before traffic starts.

Measure profit, not likes

Track contribution margin after product, shipping, platform and advertising costs.

Build bundles

Pair complementary products to increase average order value without relying only on new customers.

Retarget visitors

Use compliant remarketing to reconnect with people who viewed products or started checkout.

Keep testing

Change one major variable at a time: offer, creative, audience, landing page or price.
